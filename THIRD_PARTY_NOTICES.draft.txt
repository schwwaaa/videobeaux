======================================================
BUNDLED FONTS (videobeaux/assets/fonts/)
======================================================

Videobeaux bundles open-licensed fonts so captions look the same on every computer. They are unmodified copies from the
google/fonts repository (https://github.com/google/fonts) at commit e7db6ca603fd4862010383ff17ffd6cc4ffac1c0, fetched by tools/fetch-fonts.py; the exact
URL and SHA-256 of every file are in videobeaux/assets/fonts/SOURCES.txt. Each family's full licence text is shipped next to the
fonts, in videobeaux/assets/fonts/licenses/ — keep that folder with the fonts in every distribution.
